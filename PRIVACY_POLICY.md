# Privacy Policy for BP Wallet

**Effective Date:** August 9, 2026

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to **BP Wallet**. This Privacy Policy is provided by **Technical SA** ("we," "us," or "our") to explain how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to maintaining the trust of our users and ensuring the security of your data.

## 2. Information We Collect
To provide a functional and secure wallet management experience, we collect the following categories of data:

*   **Personal Information**: We collect your **full name**, **email address**, and **WhatsApp/phone number** during registration and account management.
*   **Photos and Videos**: Our app requires access to your device’s camera and gallery to allow you to upload **payment receipts and transaction proofs**. This is essential for verifying deposit requests and processing user actions.
*   **Device and Network Data**: We collect information related to your device and network connection to maintain app stability and security. All such data is encrypted in transit.

## 3. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected data strictly for the following purposes:
*   Facilitating wallet transactions (deposits and withdrawals).
*   Verifying payment proofs to ensure the integrity of the financial system.
*   Providing 24/7 customer support and responding to user inquiries.
*   Enhancing the security of your account and preventing fraudulent activity.

## 4. Data Sharing
**We do not share, sell, or disclose your personal data to third-party organizations.** Your information is used exclusively by Technical SA to provide the services requested within the BP Wallet application.

## 5. Data Security
The security of your data is our highest priority. We implement robust security measures, including:
*   **Encryption in Transit**: All communication between the app and our servers is encrypted using secure network protocols (HTTPS/SSL).
*   **Access Control**: Access to user data is strictly limited to authorized personnel required to process transactions and support requests.

## 6. Data Deletion and Retention
We retain your information only for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations.
*   **Right to Delete**: You have the right to request the deletion of your account and all associated personal data.
*   **How to Request Deletion**: You can request data deletion by contacting our support team directly within the app or by using the developer contact link provided in the Google Play Store listing.

## 7. Children's Privacy
BP Wallet is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. We will notify you of any significant changes by posting the new policy within the app.
